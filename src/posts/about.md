This is neither a resume not a professional bio. Just a glimpse into who I am,
what keeps me curious, inspired and sometimes a little restless.

## Who I am

<Gallery images={[{src:'https://storage.googleapis.com/commitsovercoffee/jbpvu.jpg',alt:'Sourav holding a pigeon with both hands.'}]} />

My name is Sourav Singh, and I go by the handle `@commitsovercoffee` on the web.
I'm a software developer by trade and a tinkerer  by temperament.

I live in [Ranchi](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/23.3700/85.3670&layers=T),
a quiet city in eastern India called the city of waterfalls. I’ve spent most of my
life here, but my roots trace back to [Gidhaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gidhaur_chieftaincy),
a former princely state I still visit every summer for its mango groves.

I’m a proud 90s kid who prefers a quiet, laid-back life. My thoughts align with
stoic philosophy and I don't practice any religion. Coffee keeps me going on dull
days, and I'm always up for some mutton biryani.

Growing up, I spent countless hours playing videos games and tinkering with my computer,
sometimes "ricing" it (slang for customizing) to show off at school, other times
just trying to make it run the game I desperately wanted to play.

I still remember installing Ubuntu from a CD and flexing Unity (the default desktop environment then)
as the ultimate rice. Or the time I learned to setup LANs from my computer teacher,
under the noble pretext of "learning networking", all just so I could play multiplayer
games with my friends. That curiosity kept snowballing, and over the years it
shaped me into who I am today.

## What I do

Fast forwad ~ now, I've been a software developer for nearly five years. I currently work at
[Quest Alliance](https://www.questalliance.net/), a non profit in the education
space, where I focus on design, development and support. As part of the edtech team,
I wear many hats and work with all kinds of stakeholders, which keeps things interesting.

Before that, I used to work as a freelancer (mostly) building things for the web ~ writing apis,
cli tools, admin panels, dashboards and so on. For a short time, I also helped develop
introductory coding courses, which were taught in government schools across three states in India.

I've worked remotely for most of my career (thanks to covid) and still do, though
every few months I travel for work or just a change of pace.

When I’m not working, I’m probably cycling around town, reading psychology, tinkering
with side [projects](/blog/projects) or [yak shaving](https://sketchplanations.com/yak-shaving).
And when life needs a break, I indulge in video games. I also write sometimes to
clear my head, some of which ends up on this site. I’m rarely idle ~ there’s
always a thread to pull, a system to tweak, or a movie ending to overanalyze.

## Where I am headed

So far, I’ve spent my time building features and shaping user experiences. Next,
I want to lead projects and eventually create something of my own. I plan to keep
working and building in public ~ sharing openly, learning out loud, and
growing along the way.

Outside of work, I follow no master plan (despite what my calendar might suggest), just
a compass ~ live simply, stay curious, and season life with a little sarcasm.

So yeah, that's me.
