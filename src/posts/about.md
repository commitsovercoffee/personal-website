## Who I am

I am Sourav Singh, known online as @commitsovercoffee ~ a software developer by
trade & a tinkerer by temperament. I live in [Ranchi](https://www.openstreetmap.org/#map=12/23.3700/85.3670&layers=T),
a quiet city in eastern India surrounded by waterfalls and greenery, though my
roots trace back to [Gidhaur](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gidhaur_chieftaincy),
a former princely state. I'm a proud '90s kid, 5'10", no tattoos (yet), and someone
who prefers a quiet and laidback life.

Growing up, I spent countless hours playing videos games and tinkering with my computer,
sometimes trying to "rice" it for bragging rights at school, other times just trying to
make it run the game I desperately wanted to play. I still remember installing
ubuntu (from a cd) for the first time cause that was the next level rice.

Or the time I learned to make crossover ethernet cables and setup LANs from my
computer teacher under the noble pretext of "learning networking", all just so
I could play multiplayer games with my friends. That curiosity kept snowballing,
and over the years it shaped me into who I am today.

## What I do

Fast forward to now, and I've been a software develoepr for nearly five years.
I currently work at [Quest Alliance](https://www.questalliance.net/), a non profit
working in the education space, where I mainly focus on design, development
(mostly frontend, sometimes backend) and support. I work as a contractor, which
means I get to wear many hats and work with many stakeholders, which keeps things interesting.

Before that I used to work as a freelancer, mostly building things for the web.
For a short time, I also helped develop introductory coding courses in python and
arduino. Those courses eventually made their way into government schools across
three states in India ~ a small but rewarding detour.

I've worked remotely for most of my career (thanks to covid) and still do, though
every few months I travel for work or just a change of pace.

When I’m not working, I’m probably cycling around, tinkering with side [projects](/blog/projects),
devouring a book (if the mood strikes), or escaping into video games
(if life starts to feel too heavy). I also write sometimes to clear my head.
I'm rarely idle ~ there's always a thread to pull, a system to tweak or a movie
ending to overanalyze.

Thankfully I've never been on social media (I despise short form content), which
probably explains why I still find plenty of time for the things above.

## Where I am headed

I’ve spent my time building features and shaping user experiences, and now I’m
aiming to lead projects and eventually create something of my own. Outside of work,
I don’t follow a master plan, just a compass ~ live simply, stay curious and season
life with a little sarcasm.
