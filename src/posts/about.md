## About

I am Sourav, known online as @commitsovercoffee — a software developer by trade and a tinkerer by temperament.
I live in Ranchi (the city of waterfalls), though my roots trace back to Gidhaur, a former princely state in Bihar. I’m a proud ’90s kid, 5’10”, no tattoos (yet), and someone who prefers a life that’s simple, quiet, and free.

Growing up, I spent countless hours playing video games and tinkering with my computer — sometimes trying to “rice” it for bragging rights at school, other times just trying to make it run the game I desperately wanted to play. I still remember the day I installed Ubuntu (from a CD) because that was the next-level rice. Or the time I learned to make crossover ethernet cables and set up LANs from my teacher under the noble pretext of “learning networking,” all just so I could play multiplayer games with my friends.

That curiosity kept snowballing, and over the years it shaped me into who I am today: a developer at work, a tinkerer at heart, still happiest when taking things apart.

## Work

Fast-forward to now, I've been a software developer for nearly five years. I currently work at __Quest Alliance__, a non profit working in the education space, where I mainly focus on design, development ( __mostly frontend, sometimes backend__ ) and support. Being a consultant means I wear many hats and work with many stakeholders, which keeps things interesting and grounded.

I occasionally help organize or mentor at hackathons themed around computational thinking for high school students. It's a refreshing reminder of how wonder begins, and why the work matters.

I've worked remotely for most of my career (thanks, covid), which gave me the freedom to learn, experiment, and chart my own course. Every few months I travel for meetups, workshops, or simply a change of scenery, then return to my quiet routines.

## Hobby

When I'm not working I am probably cycling around town, working on a side project, devouring books (if mood strikes) or indulging in videos games if life starts to feel too much. I also write sometimes to clear my head. I rarely sit idle, there's always a thread to pull or a system to tweak or a movie ending to discuss at length.

## Philosophy

My thoughts lean towards stoic philosophy ~ accept what is, act on what can be changed, and let the rest pass. I don't practice any religion. Liberty and peace of mind matter more to me than almost anything else. I try to live minimally, stay curious, and season life with a little sarcasm.

## Closing

I like to think from first principles: break things down to their core, understand the rules, then figure out what works for me. That mindset applies to code, projects, and life alike. It’s how I stay curious, free, and mostly sane.
